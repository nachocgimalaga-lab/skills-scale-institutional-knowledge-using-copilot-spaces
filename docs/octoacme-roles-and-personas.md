# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Core Delivery Roles

### Developers

#### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

#### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Support QA and release activities

#### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

#### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed
- Collaboration with QA Lead on test coverage and acceptance

#### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Clarifies requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on timeline and dependencies
- **QA Lead**: Coordinates testing and defect resolution
- **Technical Writer**: Provides documentation requirements
- **Automation Engineer**: Works on test automation and CI/CD improvements

---

### Product Managers

#### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

#### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics
- Partner with UX/UI Designer on user experience requirements
- Support Release Manager with feature communication

#### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs
- Design reviews with UX/UI Designer

#### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Aligns on scope, timeline, and resourcing
- **Developers**: Collaborates on feasibility and design
- **UX/UI Designer**: Defines user experience and design requirements
- **QA Lead**: Agrees on acceptance criteria and test approach
- **Support/Customer Success**: Incorporates customer feedback and issues
- **Technical Writer**: Provides product documentation requirements

---

### Project Managers

#### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

#### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication
- Escalate blockers and risks through defined channels
- Support retrospectives and continuous improvement

#### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

#### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation
- Daily standup facilitation

#### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on priorities and scope
- **Developers**: Tracks progress and identifies blockers
- **QA Lead**: Coordinates testing and release readiness
- **Release Manager**: Plans deployment and communications
- **All roles**: Facilitates communication and alignment

## Supporting & Specialized Roles

### QA Lead

#### Role Summary
QA Leads oversee quality assurance activities, coordinate testing efforts (manual and automated), and ensure features meet acceptance criteria before release. They bridge developers, product managers, and users to maintain quality standards.

#### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and quality acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Identify, prioritize, and track defects
- Ensure acceptance criteria are met before release
- Collaborate on test automation strategy with Automation Engineer
- Communicate quality risks and blockers
- Support release activities and post-release verification
- Contribute to retrospectives on quality lessons learned

#### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases with minimal production issues
- Reduce cycle time for defect resolution
- Establish clear, measurable quality standards
- Maximize test coverage and automation efficiency

#### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews with Developers and Product Manager
- Daily or weekly QA status updates
- Defect tracking and triage meetings
- Release readiness checklists and sign-offs
- Test automation planning with Automation Engineer

#### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Collaborates on test coverage, bug reproduction, and fixes
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on acceptance criteria and priorities
- **Project Manager**: Reports on quality risks and release readiness
- **Automation Engineer**: Partners on test automation and CI integration
- **Release Manager**: Provides release readiness assessment
- **Support/Customer Success**: Receives customer-reported issues

---

### UX/UI Designer

#### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers create user-centered designs and prototypes that solve customer problems and meet business objectives. They work with product managers, developers, and users to ensure excellent user experience and accessibility.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Design user interfaces and interaction flows
- Create wireframes, mockups, and prototypes
- Collaborate with developers on feasibility and implementation
- Ensure designs meet accessibility standards (WCAG)
- Review designs with stakeholders and incorporate feedback
- Establish and maintain design systems and standards
- Support QA in usability testing

#### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce user friction and support burden through good design
- Establish consistent design language and patterns
- Maximize user satisfaction and adoption

#### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Manager and Developers
- Usability testing sessions with users
- Design documentation and component libraries
- Collaboration on accessibility requirements
- Feedback integration and iteration cycles

#### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on user needs and business goals
- **Developers**: Ensures technical feasibility and implementation quality
- **Technical Writer**: Coordinates on user-facing terminology and help content
- **QA Lead**: Supports usability testing and user acceptance testing
- **Support/Customer Success**: Incorporates user feedback and pain points

---

### Technical Writer

#### Role Summary
Technical Writers create clear, accurate documentation for features, APIs, and processes. They work with developers, product managers, and support teams to ensure all stakeholders have the information they need.

#### Responsibilities
- Create user guides, API documentation, and release notes
- Maintain internal process documentation
- Collaborate with developers on technical accuracy
- Work with Product Manager on messaging and positioning
- Update documentation during feature development (shift-left approach)
- Ensure consistency in tone, terminology, and format
- Support knowledge management and searchability
- Review and update docs post-release based on user feedback

#### Goals
- Reduce support burden through clear, accessible documentation
- Enable user self-service and faster onboarding
- Maintain accurate, up-to-date knowledge base
- Support product adoption and user success

#### Typical Communication
- Documentation requirements from Product Manager and Developers
- Technical review sessions with engineering leads
- User feedback from Support team
- Documentation status and milestone updates
- Content calendars and publication planning

#### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Provides technical details and API specifications
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on user messaging and terminology
- **Support/Customer Success**: Incorporates user questions and feedback
- **QA Lead**: Reviews documentation accuracy
- **UX/UI Designer**: Coordinates on user terminology and help content

---

### Release Manager

#### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the deployment process, manage change communications, plan rollback strategies, and ensure smooth transitions to production. They work across teams to minimize risk and maximize communication during releases.

#### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate deployment windows
- Manage release checklists and pre-release verification
- Communicate release timing and impact to stakeholders
- Develop and document rollback and incident response plans
- Coordinate communication to customers and support teams
- Conduct post-release reviews and incident retrospectives
- Track deployment metrics and success criteria
- Coordinate with Support/Customer Success on launch activities

#### Goals
- Ensure smooth, predictable production releases
- Minimize deployment risk and downtime
- Maintain clear communication with all stakeholders
- Enable rapid incident response and recovery

#### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with Product and Project Managers
- Pre-deployment verification checklists
- Release notes and customer communications
- Post-deployment retrospectives and metrics reviews
- Incident response coordination

#### Key Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates timeline and dependencies
- **Developers**: Ensures code readiness and provides technical context
- **QA Lead**: Verifies release readiness and supports smoke testing
- **Support/Customer Success**: Communicates changes and coordinates support
- **Automation Engineer**: Works on deployment automation and rollback scripts

---

### Support / Customer Success

#### Role Summary
Support and Customer Success teams are the voice of the customer. They communicate production issues, escalate bugs and enhancement requests, relay user feedback, and bridge the gap between users and the product team.

#### Responsibilities
- Receive and triage customer-reported issues
- Escalate production incidents to development team
- Document and relay customer feedback to Product Manager
- Communicate known issues and workarounds to customers
- Participate in release communications and customer updates
- Track customer satisfaction and product usage trends
- Provide input on feature prioritization based on user needs
- Contribute to troubleshooting and root cause analysis

#### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce resolution time for customer issues
- Drive feature prioritization based on customer impact
- Foster strong customer relationships and advocacy

#### Typical Communication
- Customer support tickets and incident reports
- Feedback summaries for Product Manager
- Release notes and customer communications
- Escalations to development team and Developers
- Customer feedback presentations and trends analysis

#### Key Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Reports customer feedback and feature requests
- **Developers**: Escalates bugs and provides context from customers
- **QA Lead**: Reports customer-identified quality issues
- **Release Manager**: Coordinates release communications
- **Project Manager**: Alerts to critical customer-impacting issues
- **Technical Writer**: Suggests documentation improvements based on questions

### Automation Engineer

#### Role Summary
Automation Engineers build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, test automation frameworks, and deployment tooling. They enable faster, safer delivery by reducing manual work and improving quality signals.

#### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Develop and maintain automated test frameworks
- Improve build performance and reliability
- Automate deployment and rollback processes
- Monitor pipeline health and troubleshoot failures
- Collaborate with QA on test automation strategy
- Reduce manual work through scripting and automation
- Document automation tools and processes

#### Goals
- Enable rapid, safe delivery cycles
- Reduce manual toil and human error
- Improve visibility into code quality and test coverage
- Accelerate feedback to developers

#### Typical Communication
- CI/CD pipeline status and performance metrics
- Test automation planning and architecture reviews
- Collaboration on test coverage and strategy
- Documentation and runbooks for automation tooling
- Performance optimization recommendations

#### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Supports troubleshooting of pipeline and test failures
- **QA Lead**: Partners on test automation strategy and coverage
- **Release Manager**: Ensures deployment automation is reliable
- **Project Manager**: Provides visibility into pipeline health and bottlenecks

---

### Security Analyst

#### Role Summary
Security Analysts review code and dependencies for security vulnerabilities, coordinate security incident response, and advise teams on security best practices. They work to build security into the development process.

#### Responsibilities
- Conduct security code reviews and architecture reviews
- Manage vulnerability scanning and dependency checks
- Identify and recommend security improvements
- Coordinate security incident response and remediation
- Advise on authentication, encryption, and data protection
- Maintain security runbooks and incident procedures
- Support security training and awareness
- Track security metrics and compliance requirements

#### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities from reaching production
- Enable rapid incident response and recovery
- Build security awareness across the team
- Maintain compliance with security standards

#### Typical Communication
- Security review findings and recommendations
- Vulnerability reports and remediation plans
- Security incident coordination and response
- Security best practices and guidance
- Compliance and audit reporting

#### Key Interactions
- **Developers**: Provides security guidance and reviews
- **Product Manager**: Advises on security features and requirements
- **Project Manager**: Escalates critical security risks
- **QA Lead**: Coordinates security testing
- **Automation Engineer**: Ensures security scanning in CI/CD

## How These Personas Work Together

### Typical Project Workflow

1. **Initiation & Planning** (Product Manager, Project Manager, Stakeholders)
   - Product Manager defines the problem and success metrics
   - Project Manager creates the plan and timeline
   - Team reviews requirements for feasibility

2. **Design & Requirements** (UX/UI Designer, Product Manager, Developers, Security Analyst)
   - UX/UI Designer creates wireframes and prototypes
   - Product Manager refines acceptance criteria
   - Developers and Security Analyst provide technical input
   - Technical Writer plans documentation needs

3. **Development & Testing** (Developers, QA Lead, Automation Engineer, Technical Writer)
   - Developers implement features with tests
   - Automation Engineer ensures CI/CD quality gates
   - QA Lead coordinates testing and defect triage
   - Technical Writer creates or updates documentation

4. **Release Preparation** (Release Manager, QA Lead, Developers, Support/Customer Success)
   - Release Manager coordinates the deployment plan
   - QA Lead verifies release readiness
   - Developers provide technical context and support
   - Support/Customer Success prepares customer communications

5. **Retrospective** (Project Manager, All roles)
   - Project Manager facilitates the retrospective
   - All roles share learnings and improvements
   - Action items assigned with clear ownership
